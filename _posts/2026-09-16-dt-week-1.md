This was my first week working as an Application Developer at Disco Tray Studios! I am continuing my work from last April and May, developing a portal allowing the Arkansas Civic Action Network to oversee volunteering projects for High School students in central Arkansas. This week, I fixed a bug, which would have allowed students to approve their own hours. I also began the process of adding an "Archives" functionality, which will allow admin to remove old projects from the active work space without deleting them entirely.
